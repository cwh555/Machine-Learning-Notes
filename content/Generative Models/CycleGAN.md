## Overview
### Background
In the image-to-image translation task, we need paired training data. However, in many task, this is not available.

### Breakthrough
propose a method that does not need paired training data.

### Abstract
Using a GAN-based framework, an image is translated from one domain to another (e.g., summer to winter, horse to zebra), enabling the model to learn the transformation even without paired one-to-one training data.

## Methods
Goal: learn mapping functions between domain $X$, $Y$, given training samples $\{x_i\}_{i = 1}^M$, $\{y_j\}_{j = 1}^N$.

models include:
- $G$: generator, $X \rightarrow Y$
- $F$: generator, $Y \rightarrow X$
- discriminator $D_X$: to distinguish whether the input is real data $X$ or generated by $F$.
- discriminator $D_Y$: to distinguish whether the input is real data $Y$ or generated by $G$.

#### Cycle Consistency Loss
If the neural network is very large, it may produce arbitrary mappings while still achieving a low training loss. To address this, we observe that a good mapping should satisfy $G(F(x)) \approx x$ and vice versa. Therefore, a **cycle consistency loss** is defined.

## Experiments
### Training Details
- *optimizer*: Adam
- *batchsize*: 1
- *lr*: 0.0002
- *scheduler*: keep the same learning rate for the first 100 epochs and linearly decay the rate to zero over the next 100 epochs.

### Discriminator Loss
The original GAN uses negative log likelihood, but here it is replaced with a least squares loss, which enables more stable training and higher-quality generated images.

![[截圖 2025-12-27 下午5.16.31.png#center|500]]

### Reduce Model Oscillation
*Problem*: During GAN training, the generator is constantly changing, causing the discriminator to oscillate.

*Shrivastava et al.’s strategy*: Instead of using only the latest generated images to train the discriminator, maintain a buffer of the most recent 50 generated images and randomly sample from it to update the discriminator.

*Effect*: Reduces oscillation and stabilizes training.

## Discussion
### Future Works
CycleGAN performs well for color/style transfer, but struggles in the following scenarios:

- **Geometric structure changes**:  
    For example, dog → cat translation. The model only makes minor adjustments and cannot truly alter shapes.
    
- **Dataset characteristics issues:**  
    For example, horse → zebra. If the training data comes from ImageNet wild horse/zebra images without cases like “person riding a horse,” the model fails completely in such situations.
    
- **Semantic ambiguity:**  
    For example, photo → label translation. The model may confuse “tree” and “building.” Without paired data, it is difficult to resolve semantic ambiguities.

*Potential solution*: Incorporate weak supervision or semi-supervision to address these limitations.
