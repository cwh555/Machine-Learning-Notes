### Gaussian Parzen window-based log-likelihood estimates
This is an early evaluation method for implicit generative models. However, the reported performance is largely dominated by the choice of the bandwidth hyperparameter, leading to highly unstable and unreliable scores. As a result, this evaluation method has largely been discarded by the research community.

Used in *GAN*, *Deep GSN*, *DBN / RBM samples*.
